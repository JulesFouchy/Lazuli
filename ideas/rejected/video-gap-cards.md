# Gap cards in the exported video

**Rejected.** Considered inserting a brief card reading "12 days later" between entries whose journal dates are not consecutive, mirroring the timeline's gap connectors.

## Why not

The video is meant to be the "one second every day" form: entries back to back, nothing between them. Title cards break that rhythm and lengthen a video whose whole appeal is being short.

The gaps are not lost. **The day counter carries them** — `Day 12` following `Day 4` shows the pause on its own, at no cost in runtime, which is why the counter is drawn on every frame.

## If this comes back

The thing that would justify it is wanting the video to narrate rather than flow — a year-in-review with chapters. That is a different artefact from the summary video, and would be better as a separate export mode than as a flag on this one.
